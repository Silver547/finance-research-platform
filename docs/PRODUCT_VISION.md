# PRODUCT BIBLE

## Product Name

Market Intelligence Platform

---

# Vision

Explain markets, don't just report them.

---

# Mission

Build an AI-powered platform that converts global and Indian news into clear, actionable market intelligence.

The platform should help users understand:

- What happened
- Why it happened
- How it affects India
- Which sectors are affected
- Which companies are affected
- What to watch next

without reading dozens of articles.

---

# Target Users

- MBA Students
- Retail Investors
- Financial Analysts
- Portfolio Managers
- Researchers

---

# Product Principles

1. Understand today's market in under 30 seconds.

2. Every page answers a clear question.

3. AI explains rather than summarizes.

4. Show only information that helps decision making.

5. Remove clutter.

6. Quality over quantity.

---

# Core Pages

- Today
- Companies
- Industries
- Macro
- Research

---

# Long-Term Vision

Become a complete Market Intelligence Platform instead of a news dashboard.
