# PRODUCT VISION

# Market Intelligence Platform

> **Explain markets, don't just report them.**

---

# Executive Summary

The Market Intelligence Platform is an AI-powered research and decision-support platform designed to help users understand how global and Indian events affect financial markets.

Instead of simply aggregating financial news, the platform transforms scattered information into structured market intelligence by connecting events with their economic impact, affected sectors, companies, and macroeconomic trends.

The platform is designed to answer the questions that investors, analysts, researchers, and students ask every morning:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- Why does it matter?
- How does it affect India?
- Which industries are impacted?
- Which companies should I investigate?
- What should I research next?

The objective is not to replace professional terminals like Bloomberg or Reuters.

The objective is to make market intelligence understandable, explainable, and accessible.

---

# Vision

Create an AI-powered Market Intelligence Platform that explains markets instead of simply reporting them.

The platform should help users understand complex financial events quickly while preserving analytical depth.

---

# Mission

Transform global and Indian financial information into structured intelligence that can be understood in less than one minute.

Instead of overwhelming users with hundreds of articles, the platform should identify:

- The important events
- Their causes
- Their consequences
- Their market impact
- Their relationship with India's economy

---

# Problem Statement

Today's financial information is fragmented.

Users consume information from:

- Reuters
- CNBC
- Moneycontrol
- Bloomberg
- Economic Times
- Company filings
- Government releases
- Central bank announcements

Every source explains only one part of the story.

Very few platforms explain:

- how events are connected,
- how they affect India,
- which industries benefit,
- which companies are exposed,
- and what should be researched next.

This platform attempts to bridge that gap.

---

# Product Philosophy

The platform follows five core principles.

## 1. Explain before reporting

News alone has limited value.

Every important event should answer:

- Why did it happen?
- Why does it matter?
- What happens next?

---

## 2. Reduce information overload

Users should not need to read dozens of articles.

The platform should reduce complexity while preserving important information.

---

## 3. AI should explain

AI is used to:

- connect information,
- summarize intelligently,
- prioritize events,
- identify relationships,
- explain consequences.

AI should never generate unnecessary text simply because it can.

---

## 4. India-first perspective

Most financial platforms explain global markets.

This platform emphasizes:

"What does this mean for India?"

Every major event should eventually connect to:

- India's economy
- industries
- companies
- investors

---

## 5. Research, not recommendations

The platform exists to improve understanding.

It does not provide:

- Buy recommendations
- Sell recommendations
- Trading signals
- Investment advice

Users make their own decisions.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature must satisfy these principles.

## Understand within 30 seconds

The dashboard should communicate the market quickly.

---

## Every card has one purpose

Avoid cards that mix unrelated information.

---

## Visuals before paragraphs

Users scan before they read.

Information should be visual whenever possible.

---

## Progressive disclosure

Show the summary first.

Allow users to drill deeper only when they want more information.

---

## Trust over quantity

One accurate insight is more valuable than twenty weak insights.
