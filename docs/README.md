# Market Intelligence Platform

> **Explain markets, don't just report them.**

An AI-powered market intelligence platform that transforms global and Indian financial news into structured, explainable market intelligence.

Instead of simply summarizing headlines, the platform helps users understand:

- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- How does it affect India?
- Which sectors and companies are impacted?
- What are the biggest risks and opportunities?
- What should you research next?

The platform combines AI-powered news analysis, macroeconomic data, company intelligence, sector analysis, and research workflows into a single dashboard designed for investors, analysts, researchers, and students.

> **This is a research and decision-support platform—not a trading signal generator.** It is designed to help users understand markets, not provide buy or sell recommendations.
